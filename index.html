Fire Flipper

Journey to the Celestial City: a free 3D arcade game that you play in your browser. No download and no sign-up.

Guide a bird through five painted worlds on a 10,000 metre journey. Dodge rocks and flying hazards, collect glowing orbs, use power-ups and earn rewards on the way to the Celestial City.

Five worlds

  • Red Rock Canyon
  • Frosty Polar
  • Celestial Realm
  • Rose Sky Palace
  • Cloud Sanctuary

How to play

  • Tap, click or press Space to flap your wings.
  • Hold to climb higher, release to fall; on a keyboard press Down or S to dive.
  • Fly through the gaps, collect orbs, and grab the magnet and shield power-ups.
  • Watch the Auto play or the full Journey Preview first if you want to see how it is done.

Features

  • Five 2,000 metre worlds, each with its own scenery, obstacles and hazards
  • Flying hazards that arrive one new kind per world
  • Five birds, each in three skins, plus journey rewards
  • Works on desktop and on phones, in landscape or portrait
